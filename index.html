A 1V1 CARD BATTLER

SCORING — Score = (hand chips + card chips) × multiplier. Jokers inflate that number fast.
DAMAGE — Score ÷ 95 (rounded up) hits your opponent directly. First to drop them from 150 HP wins.
REWARD — Score 160+ in one play and you draft 1 of 3 Jokers as a permanent upgrade (once per turn, 5 slots max).
CONTROLS — Click cards to select (max 5), then PLAY. Not sure? Hit BEST and the game picks your strongest combo.
